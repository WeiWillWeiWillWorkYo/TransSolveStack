# License Policy

Copyright (c) 2026 Wei CUI.

TransSolveStack uses a non-permissive public release route. The project is not
released under Apache-2.0, MIT, BSD, or a similar permissive license.

## Selected Route

- Source code: PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0, with a required notice
  preserving attribution to Wei CUI and TransSolveStack.
- Commercial use: separate written permission from Wei CUI is required.
- Documentation, reports, footage, and paper-facing evidence: CC BY-NC 4.0
  direction, preserving attribution and noncommercial use.
- Trained selector models and weights: noncommercial open-weight contribution
  terms in `MODEL_CONTRIBUTION_TERMS.md`.
- Contributions: CLA-light terms in `CONTRIBUTOR_LICENSE_AGREEMENT.md`.

## Current Intent

TransSolveStack should allow legitimate noncommercial research use,
reproducibility checks, and contributions back to the project, especially:

- GPU solver and preconditioner implementations.
- SuiteSparse profiling and benchmark artifacts.
- Transformer selector training experiments and model cards.
- Guarded runtime policy and fallback safety improvements.

The project should not be presented as someone else's original work. Public use
must preserve attribution to Wei CUI and the TransSolveStack project.

## Commercial Boundary

Commercial use is not automatically granted. Commercial product integration,
commercial redistribution, paid hosted service use, or proprietary consulting
packaging based on TransSolveStack requires separate written permission from
Wei CUI.

## Upload Gate

The license route is selected, but public GitHub upload still requires a final
manual review of:

- `LICENSE`
- `COMMERCIAL_USE.md`
- `MODEL_CONTRIBUTION_TERMS.md`
- `CONTRIBUTOR_LICENSE_AGREEMENT.md`
- generated public-release hygiene artifacts
